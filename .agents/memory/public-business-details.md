Do not equate a registered office or a directory listing with the current public business location. Obtain the user's approval before adding business contact details or official profile links. The user has approved the business phone and address in structured data, but asked that they not appear visibly on the Contact page.

**Why:** Public sources can refer to different kinds of addresses and can be outdated. The user explicitly requested that the phone and address remain in schema while being removed from the visible Contact page.

**How to apply:** For these approved details, retain the phone and address in the business schema and omit them from visible Contact-page content. Verify accuracy and permission before adding other public details or profile links.
