---
name: Public business details
description: Approval and visibility requirements for business contact schema.
---

Do not equate a registered office or a directory listing with the current public business location. Obtain the user's approval before adding business contact details or official profile links.

**Why:** Public sources can refer to different kinds of addresses and can be outdated. The user approved the researched business contact details and a matching contact-page addition, but supplied no official profile links.

**How to apply:** For future changes, verify both accuracy and permission to publish. Keep contact details in structured data aligned with visible contact information; Google's guidance does not support hiding these facts only in markup. The approved contact-page addition is not permission for unrelated visible SEO changes.
